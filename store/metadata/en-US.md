## Description (max 4000)
Calm Moments is a quiet app for meditation and sleep. The scene moves with your breath: as you inhale the tree lifts, the aurora brightens and the campfire grows; as you exhale everything settles. No account needed — just open it and begin.

BREATHE WITH THE SCENE
• Breathing patterns: natural, resonant, box breathing, 4-7-8 relax, or unguided
• A large breathing circle and gentle prompts, easy to follow without staring
• 3, 5, 10, 20 minutes or open-ended, with a singing bowl to close
• Optional vibration and phase chimes

13 MEDITATION SCENES
Season Tree, Seaside Tide, Rainy Window and Candlelight, plus Sea Lookout, Lighthouse, Whale Song, Mountain Clouds, Spirit Deer, Lotus Pond, Snowy Cabin, Campfire and Aurora Night in the Full Version. Each has its own day and night, its own sounds, and rises and falls with your breath.

18 AMBIENT SOUNDS TO MIX
Wind, rain, stream, waves, birds, crickets, candle, wind chimes, campfire, distant thunder, whale song, gulls, foghorn, temple bell, drone pad, white, pink and brown noise. All synthesised live on your phone — no internet, no audible loops.

SLEEP MODE
Keeps playing with the screen off. Choose 15 to 90 minutes and the sound slowly fades out.

LOG & REMINDERS
Streaks, total time, a weekly view and a daily reminder at the time you choose.

PRIVACY
No sign-up. Your practice log stays on your phone, and an ad only appears when you choose to watch one.

FREE & FULL VERSION
Four scenes, every sound, the breathing guide, sleep mode and the log are free. Curious about another scene? Watch one ad to try it free for a day (up to 5 a day).
The Full Version is a one-time purchase that unlocks the other 9 scenes and every future scene, with no ads ever. No subscription.

This app is for relaxation only and is not a substitute for professional medical or mental-health advice.
